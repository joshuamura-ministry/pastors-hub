One-time setup: a free Census key

The Census Bureau requires a key on every request. It's free, takes two minutes, and never expires.

  1. Request a key at api.census.gov/data/key_signup.html (any organisation name, your email).
  2. Open index.html, find CENSUS_KEY near the top of the script, paste the key between the quotes, redeploy.

Find your conference

Tap your state on the map, or search the list. Then enter the access code for your conference.

Codes are issued per person, so one can be withdrawn without disturbing anyone else. If your conference is not yet enrolled, the screen will tell you who to ask.
